How Daemon Works

Daemon is a twisted take on 5e set in Night City. Every merc is a 5e Fighter and keeps all of its normal Fighter features.

There are no classes and no feats. Instead every merc fills a role, and what really makes your merc yours is the set of perk trees you climb with your stats. Those trees are ported from Cyberpunk 2077.

Double proficiency

You can only get double proficiency after moving two thresholds. A Rookie Netrunner can be proficient in Hacking, but they can't double it until they reach Phenom.

Every level after 1 gives a perk point. Yellow levels also give 2 stat increases.

Your Turn

Normal 5e gives you a movement, an action and a bonus action. Daemon gives you 2 actions per turn and you spend them however you want.

MoveAttackGrappleReloadHealThrowUse a skill, perk or objectHold an action

Reactions still exist but work a little differently. See below.

Reactions

You get 1 reaction per round, same as 5e, and every reaction costs 2 RAM.

ReactionEffect
BraceAdd your TEC mod to your AC
SlideMake a REF save against a DC to take half damage
RebootMake an INT check against a DC to give the attacker disadvantage

RAM

RAM is the fuel for reactions, hacks and some perks. Your max RAM is 4 + your INT mod, plus whatever your installed cyberware adds.

The INT perk Extra Slot doubles your max RAM, and Automation cuts every RAM cost by 1, so reactions only cost 1.

Overheated

If your RAM hits 0 your system overheats. You're stunned and can't do anything until your RAM comes back. The INT perk Insulated Wires keeps you from ever overheating. The DM can drain or restore your RAM at any time.

Netrunning and Black ICE

To go into The Net you need the INT perk Jack In. Netrunners can run into defensive anti-personnel programs called Black ICE (Intrusion Countermeasure Electronics), and they can be lethal.

Netrunners have two HP pools, IRL HP and NET HP. They're equal unless a perk, item or hack changes that. Black ICE mostly attacks your NET HP, but your body can still be attacked while you're under. If either pool hits 0 you go unconscious in critical condition.

Cyberware and RipperDocs

Chrome goes into body slots, and each piece can add to your max RAM.

Cyberware threshold

Every piece of cyberware has a cost, set by the DM or by the Fabricator who built it. Installed chrome fills your tally. Past your threshold you're in the overflow, up to your max, and you can't install anything that would go over your max. Each point of overflow raises your Cyberpsychosis DC by 2.

Example. Threshold 10, max 15. A tally of 12 is 2 points of overflow, so your Cyberpsychosis DC is +4.

Only a merc with the TEC Legend perk RipperDoc can install or remove cyberware, in themselves or in anyone else. If nobody in the crew is a RipperDoc, you'll need the DM's ripper.

During a live session a RipperDoc sees an install panel on every party member's card. The part has to be in the patient's bag, so hand it over first. Any merc can hand items and eddies to the rest of the crew.

Hot-swappable parts

For an extra fee, a RipperDoc can make a part hot-swappable. Anyone can swap a hot part in or out of themselves without a RipperDoc, but it comes with a cost.

Price500 €$ of the RipperDoc's time and parts
Weaker linkA hot part only gives half its RAM bonus, rounded down
SwappingCosts 2 RAM and an ACTION each time you swap it in or out yourself

The hot-swap numbers are a house rule draft until the GM signs off.

Fabrication

Only a merc with the TEC Phenom perk Fabricator can create, upgrade or fortify items. Roll Craft against a DC the DM sets, then pay for the parts. To work on a crewmate's gear, they hand it to you and you hand it back.

JobCostWhat you get
Create300 €$A new item with stats agreed with the DM
Upgrade200 €$ per Mk levelMk II, Mk III, then Mk IV at most. The DM decides what each level adds
Fortify150 €$The item is reinforced and tagged FORT

The Maintenance Crew perk lets you repair items with your Craft score. These prices are a draft until the GM sets them.

Hacks and Cool Bonuses

The DM can push hacks onto a merc. A hacked merc's sheet glitches and locks up until the DM clears it. Hacks stack.

HackEffect
System LockoutCan't use reactions or spend RAM, and perk installs are locked
Weapon GlitchCan't swap weapons, and weapon attacks are at disadvantage
Optics RebootDisadvantage on attacks, Perception and Scan
Cyberware MalfunctionCyberware is offline. Its RAM bonuses are gone and nothing can be swapped
Short CircuitDisadvantage on every roll

Cool bonuses

The DM hands out Cool bonuses for great play, like inspiration in 5e. Spend one from your sheet to take advantage or a reroll. If you have the COO perk You Ready to Rock?!, spending one also gives every ally COOL buffs and a free ACTION.

The hack list is a draft until the GM finalizes hacking rules.

Cyberpsychosis

Cyberpsychosis saves are normally made at −5. A few perks push back on that, like Theseus and I'm Special in BOD, Tranquility in COO and Limit Setter in TEC. The full rules are still coming.

Perk Trees

Every perk tree is tied to one stat. If that stat is under 10 the tree is offline. Higher tiers open as the stat climbs, and you spend 1 perk point per perk.

TierStat neededPerks
Rookie10–132
Pro14–163
Phenom17–193
Legend202

Each tree is shaped like a fork, or a "W". The two outer branches run all four tiers. The middle branch opens at Pro from either Rookie perk and ends at Phenom. Each Legend perk only opens from its own branch's Phenom.
